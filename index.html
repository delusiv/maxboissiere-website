About

My name is Max Boissiere, a cinematographer based in Arizona. I shoot narrative shorts, commercials, music videos and documentaries, and I edit corporate and event work.

I grade my own work in DaVinci Resolve and write DCTLs for film emulation, most recently a simulation of Kodak VISION3 500T printed onto Kodak 2383 dye density.
